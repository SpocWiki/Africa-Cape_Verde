## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Praia** (pronunciation: [ˈpɾajɐ], Portuguese for "beach") is the capital and largest city of Cape Verde. 
> Located on the southern coast of Santiago island within the Sotavento Islands group, 
> the city is the seat of the Praia Municipality. 
> Praia is the political, economic and cultural center of Cape Verde.
>
